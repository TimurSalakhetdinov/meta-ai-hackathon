# Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon: rules summary

Sources: the [details page](https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon/details) (full text in `details-page-full.md`) and the kickoff livestream slides (`kickoff-slides/`), both from 2026-10-26.

## Timeline
- Oct 26: kickoff livestream.
- Oct 27 to Nov 4: live workshops from Meta and partners, plus office hours on Discord.
- **Submission deadline: Wed Nov 4, 9:00 PM ET (Thu Nov 5, 03:00 Berlin).** Don't wait for the last hour; aim for the evening of Nov 4 Berlin time.
- Nov 9: closing ceremony with a live final and winners announced.

## What to submit (all four)
1. A submission on the CV platform at https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon/hackathon/submit, listing every teammate.
2. A **3-minute demo video** that shows only what was built during the event. Judges score from it.
3. A **public GitHub repo**. Check that it opens in a logged-out browser before submitting.
4. All of it before the deadline.

## Track
Indie developer track: individual builders and students, teams of up to 4, judged on creativity, technical ambition and whether it works. Prizes: $150K, $100K, $75K, $50K and $25K ($400K total). The startup track ($550K) is not ours. Bonus awards of up to $50K in aggregate (for example community vote or most viral project) are at the organizers' discretion.

## Themes
1. **Build for your world**: bring Meta's models into your own domain and solve a problem you know firsthand. *This project's theme.*
2. Real world: software, robotics, manufacturing, energy, materials and physical systems.

## Judging
- Stage 1, pass/fail: does the project reasonably fit a theme and use Meta AI products?
- Stage 2, rubric scoring 0 to 10:
  - **Impact, 30%**: real-world potential, who benefits, whether people could use it, theme fit.
  - **Use of Meta AI tooling, 25%**: creative use that goes beyond a basic integration and surfaces unexpected capabilities.
  - **Technical depth and execution, 20%**: sound, refined engineering and real craft.
  - **Demo, 25%**: a working, impressive demo that holds up live, plus the quality of the video.
- Stage 3: the top 5 per track demo live on Nov 9, and judges rank them.

## Anti-projects (overdone; avoid looking like them)
AI medical advice, simple image analyzers, **dashboards as the main feature**, AI job application screeners, personality analyzers, basic RAG apps, **Streamlit apps**, "AI for Education" chatbots, AI nutrition coaches, sports analyzers or coaches. The list is not complete.

## Models and credits
- Meta Model API: Muse Spark series, Muse Wonder, Muse Image, SAM 3.1. Also Meta open-weights models and the Muse Code coding agent.
- $150 Meta Model API credits per participant, valid for the 10-day window.
- Partner credits for open-weights models, via request forms in Discord #resources: Baseten $100 plus 2M TPM (first 2,000 participants), Modal $100 (first 1,000), Fireworks $25 (select participants).

## IP and licensing
The entrant keeps ownership, but Meta and the organizers get a perpetual, royalty-free, non-exclusive license to use the submission. Open-source dependencies are allowed if their licenses are followed. The submission must be original work.
